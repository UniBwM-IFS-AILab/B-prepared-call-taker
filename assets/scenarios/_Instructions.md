## Instructions

During the call, please:

- **Stay in character** as the caller.
- Please do not perform prank calls or "play" around, the system is not (yet) designed to deal with that.
- **Use your own words**; do not just read the scenario text.
- **Speak in full sentences**, like you would in a real phone conversation.
- **Improvise** if the scenario text does not provide an answer.
<!-- - If something is missing, **make a reasonable assumption** that fits the situation. -->
- Only mention information the caller can realistically know.
- **Be patient**. In rare cases the system may take longer to respond than expected.
